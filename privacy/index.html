The short version

  • We collect what it takes to run a chat app: your account, your messages and the chats you're in.
  • Messages, translations, photos and files are encrypted on the way and before they're stored.
  • Translation runs on our own servers. Your messages are never sent to an outside translation service or AI company.
  • No ads, no selling your data, and no analytics or tracking in the app or on this website.
  • You can delete your account, with everything you've sent, at any time. Here's how.

Who we are

Olingo Chat is run by RockSon Tech ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Olingo Chat web app at app.olingo.chat, the Olingo Chat Android app, and this website, olingo.chat.

RockSon Tech is responsible for the personal information described here. If you have a question about it, email support@olingo.chat.

What we collect

Your account

  • Username, display name and language. You choose them when you sign up. Your language decides which language you read messages in.
  • Password. Stored only as an Argon2 hash, never as you typed it. Accounts made with Google, Facebook or Apple may not have one.
  • Profile picture (optional). Resized and saved as a small image.
  • Email address (optional). Only if you add one in Settings, confirmed with a 6-digit code. Only you can see it.
  • Your settings, such as who can start a chat with you and whether read receipts are on.

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple

We receive the ID that service uses for you, your name and your email address. From Facebook we ask only for your public profile and email address. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one.

We use this only to sign you in, to suggest a username and display name when you create your account, and to show you which account is connected. We never receive your password for that service, and we don't get your contacts, friends list or posts.

Passkeys

If you add a passkey, we store only its public key, the name you give it, and when it was added and last used. Your fingerprint, face or PIN stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Your messages and files

The messages you send and their translations, the photos and files you share (up to 20 MB each) and their file names, game invites, group names and chat themes. We also note which language each message was written in.

Information about your chats

Which chats you're in and when you joined them, when each message was sent, how far you've read in each chat (for read receipts and unread counts), and the people you've blocked.

Devices and sign-ins

For each device you're signed in on: a general name taken from your browser or app (for example "Chrome on Windows" or "Android app") and when you signed in. You can see this list in Settings > Devices. Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes.

Technical logs

Like most online services, our servers keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, times and the pages or features requested. We use them to keep Olingo Chat running, fix problems and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

  • To run Olingo Chat: create and secure your account, deliver your messages, translate them for each reader, and keep your chats in sync on every device.
  • To keep it safe: protect accounts, stop spam and abuse, and look into reports.
  • To contact you: only to send the code that confirms your email address. We don't send marketing email.
  • To fix problems and keep the service working well.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you for anyone else.

If you're in the EU, the UK or a country with similar laws: we use your information because we need it to provide the service you signed up for, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping Olingo Chat secure and working, because the law requires it, or, for optional things like a profile picture or an email address, because you chose to give it to us.

How translation works

When you send a message, Olingo Chat's own server works out its language and translates it into the language of each person in the chat, using an AI language model that runs on our own hardware. A few earlier messages from the same chat are included, so short replies like "me too" are translated correctly.

Your messages are never sent to an outside translation service or AI company. Translations are stored, encrypted, so the same message isn't translated twice. Anyone in the chat can tap Show original to see exactly what you wrote.

How we protect it

  • All connections to Olingo Chat use HTTPS.
  • Messages, translations, photos, files and file names are encrypted with AES-256 before they're stored. The encryption key is kept apart from the database and its backups.
  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2, and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Our admin tools list chats without their content. Opening a chat's messages, for example to look into an abuse report, needs a written reason, which is recorded before anything is shown.

Olingo Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. To translate a message, our server has to read it, so it decrypts messages to translate and deliver them.

Not encrypted when stored: usernames, display names, email addresses, profile pictures, group names, who is in each chat, and when messages were sent. The server needs them to deliver messages.

The Security section on our home page has the technical details. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Who can see your information

Other Olingo Chat users

  • Anyone signed in to Olingo Chat who knows your username can see your username, display name, language and profile picture.
  • People in a chat with you see the messages you send there, both translated and in your original words. They can also see when you're typing and, unless you turn read receipts off, when you've read their messages.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

Service providers

  • Email delivery. When we send you a confirmation code, your email address and the code pass through our email provider.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the Android app download, and may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Sign-in services

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple, that service knows you used it to sign in to Olingo Chat. Its own privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Games

Games in Olingo Chat come from XGAME (xgame.xode.net), a separate service with its own accounts and scores. Starting a game posts an invite in the chat, and opening it loads the game from XGAME. We don't send XGAME your messages or your account details. XGAME's own policies apply to what you do there.

When the law requires it

We may share information if the law requires it, or if it's needed to protect someone's safety or the security of Olingo Chat. We share only what's required.

If the business changes hands

If RockSon Tech is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of it, and it stays protected by this policy.

We never sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • Web app: essential cookies that keep you signed in. Page scripts can't read them, and they're sent only to Olingo Chat over HTTPS. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Android app: your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage, and settings like the app lock are saved on the phone.
  • This website (olingo.chat): no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.

Phone permissions

Olingo Chat asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Camera: to scan a QR code when you sign in on another device or add a device to your account.
  • Photos: to send the photos you pick, or set one as your profile picture. Olingo Chat sees only the photos you choose.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: to open the app if you turn on the app lock. Your phone does the check, and we never receive any biometric data.

Olingo Chat doesn't use your microphone, your location or your contacts. You can turn permissions off at any time in your phone's settings.

Your choices and rights

You can do most things yourself in the app:

  • Settings > Profile: change your name, photo and language.
  • Settings > Account: change your username, email address and password, connect or disconnect Google, Facebook, Apple and passkeys, and delete your account.
  • Settings > Privacy & security: choose who can start a chat with you, block people, and turn read receipts off.
  • Settings > Devices: sign out any device you don't recognise.

To get a copy of your information, have something corrected, or ask us to delete your account if you can't sign in, email support@olingo.chat. We'll reply within 30 days, and we may ask you to show that the account is yours first.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you more rights: for example, to access, correct or delete your information, to get it in a portable format, or to object to or restrict how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Olingo Chat isn't meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for using online services in their country, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child is using Olingo Chat, email support@olingo.chat and we'll delete the account.

Where your information is stored

Olingo Chat runs on servers we operate ourselves. If you use it from another country, your information is transferred to and stored where those servers are, where data protection laws may differ from yours. Wherever it's processed, we protect it as this policy describes.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in Olingo Chat or on this website before it takes effect.
