Translation

Translation that reads like a person wrote it

Oh Chat doesn't translate word by word. It reads the conversation so replies make sense, and it keeps the tone friendly.

  • Understands context

    It reads the last few messages, so short replies like "me too" and the subjects Korean and Japanese often leave out come through correctly.

  • Polite by default

    Korean translations use the friendly polite style (해요체), so nobody sounds rude by accident.

  • Leaves the right things alone

    Names, emoji, links and numbers stay as they are. "ok", "lol" and "haha" don't need a translator.

  • Never leaves you waiting

    The original arrives instantly. If a translation is ever unavailable, you still have the message and a Retry button.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need an invite?

Yes, for now. Oh Chat is invite-only while it grows. Enter the code you received when you create your account.

How good is the translation?

Oh Chat uses a modern AI model that understands context, slang and tone, and keeps names, emoji and links as they are. No translation is perfect, so the exact original is always one tap away.

Can other people see what I originally wrote?

Yes. Anyone in the chat can tap Show original to see your exact words, just as you can with theirs.

What happens if I change my language?

Go to Settings, then Profile, and pick a new language. New messages arrive in it right away, and older messages are translated when you scroll back to them.

Which devices does Oh Chat work on?

Any computer with a modern web browser, and Android phones. An iPhone app is coming. Your chats are the same on every device, and you can sign in on a computer by scanning a QR code with your phone.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK on your phone and open it. When Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install. New versions install over the old one, and your chats stay where they are.
