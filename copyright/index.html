Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe something on Olingo Chat or this website infringes your copyright, email support@olingo.chat with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

  • your name, postal address, phone number and email address;
  • a description of the work you believe has been copied;
  • where the material appears, such as the Olingo Chat username that shared it, or the address of the page on this website;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
  • a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act for them;
  • your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough).

We review every complete notice. If it's valid, we remove or disable access to the material, and we may pass your notice on to the person who shared it. We close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe copyright.

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake, or that you have the right to use it, email us with an explanation and we'll look at it again.

Please only send a notice if you're sure. Claiming that material infringes copyright when it doesn't can have legal consequences.
