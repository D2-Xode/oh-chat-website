Delete your Olingo Chat account

You can delete your Olingo Chat account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. It takes a minute in the app, or you can email us if you can't sign in.

Delete your account in the app

This works the same in the Android app and in the web app.

  1. Open Olingo Chat and sign in. On a computer, go to app.olingo.chat.
  2. In your chat list, tap your profile picture (or the person icon) at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Tap Account and scroll down to Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. If you sign in only with Google, Facebook, Apple or a passkey, type your username instead.
  5. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away, and you're signed out on every device.

Deleting your account is permanent. We can't bring back a deleted account or its messages, photos and files.

Can't sign in? Ask us to delete it

Email support@olingo.chat with the subject "Delete my Olingo Chat account" and include your username. If your account has an email address, send it from that address.

We may ask you to show that the account is yours before we delete anything, so nobody can delete someone else's account. We'll delete it within 30 days of your request and reply to tell you it's done.

If you signed in with Facebook, Google or Apple

When you sign in with one of these, Olingo Chat receives the ID that service uses for you, your name and your email address. We use them only to sign you in. Deleting your Olingo Chat account, as described above, deletes all of it.

You can also remove Olingo Chat from your account with that service, so it no longer shares anything with us:

  • Facebook: go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites, choose Olingo Chat, then tap Remove.
  • Google: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Olingo Chat, then delete the connection.
  • Apple: on your iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple, choose Olingo Chat, then stop using it.

Removing Olingo Chat there doesn't delete your Olingo Chat account or your messages. To delete those, follow the steps above.

What's deleted

  • Your profile: username, display name, profile picture, language, email address and password.
  • Your settings and the list of people you've blocked.
  • Your sign-in methods: connected Google, Facebook and Apple accounts, and your passkeys.
  • Every device you're signed in on.
  • Every message you've sent, with its translations, and every photo and file you've shared.
  • Your place in every chat. Groups with no members left are deleted completely.

What isn't deleted

  • Messages other people sent stay in their chats, because they belong to the people who sent them.
  • Groups that still have members carry on without you, with the other members' messages.
  • Your username becomes free, and someone else can choose it later.
  • Records of our team opening a chat (who, when, which chat and why, never the messages themselves) are kept as security records.
  • Server backups and technical logs may still hold copies for a limited time, until they're replaced. We don't restore deleted accounts from them.
  • Information the law requires us to keep, for example to respond to a legal request, is kept only as long as needed.

Delete only some of your data

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove some of your information:

  • Profile picture: Settings > Profile.
  • Email address: Settings > Account > Email, then Remove.
  • Facebook, Google or Apple sign-in, or a passkey: Settings > Account > Sign-in methods. You need at least one way left to sign in.
  • A group: open the group, tap the chat info button (ⓘ) at the top right, then Leave group.
  • A signed-in device: Settings > Devices.
